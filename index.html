What to search
Occupational series — one per line
Format: four-digit code, then a label. Find codes at usajobs.gov/help/faq/job-announcement/job-series. A posting in a series you do not list is never requested at all.
Keyword searches — one per line
Full-text searches run alongside the series searches. Good for roles that hide in unexpected series.
How to score
Profile keywords — one per line
Matches in the title score heavily, matches in the duties score lightly. The resume tool on the Setup tab fills this in for you.
Preferred organizations — one per line
Relocation phrases — one per line
Hidden-location signals — one per line
Terms that reveal a job is really based at your target location even when the listing shows an HQ address elsewhere.
What to reject
Excluded title words — one per line
Matched as plain substrings of the title. Pad short words with spaces so "cook" does not also reject "cookie".

Excluded degree fields — one per line
